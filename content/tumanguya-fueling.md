Sept 13 to 16. Halal, boil water only. One night at 12,000 ft, so the
15th is the only recovery window.

| day | where | eat | drink |
|---|---|---|---|
| sep 13 | Lone Pine, 3,700 ft | 2,900 | 3.5 L |
| sep 14 | Portal, 8,360 ft | 3,200 | 4 L |
| sep 15 | Trail Camp, 12,000 ft | 3,400 | 5 L |
| sep 16 | summit + out, 14,505 ft | 300-350 g carbs | 5-6 L |

## rules

- Eat by clock, not hunger. Every 45 min moving, every 2 hrs in camp.
- Carbs 60-65% of calories from the 15th on.
- 500-800 mg sodium per liter on moving days.
- 60-80 g carbs + 25 g protein within 30 min of dropping the pack.
- Nothing new above the Portal. Test everything on a training hike.
- No alcohol from the 12th.

## sep 13, drive up

- Pre drive: oats, banana, peanut butter, coffee. 600
- Lunch stop: sandwich or burrito, salted. 600
- Road: something every 2 hrs. Dates, pretzels, juice. 700
- Dinner: rice or pasta, low grease, salt heavily. 1,000
- Bed: 500 ml + electrolyte tab

## sep 14, portal

- Breakfast: eat big at the BnB. Eggs, toast, fruit. 800
- Trail: 40-60 g carbs/hr. One bar + chews. 500
- Back at camp: wrap + drink. 600
- Dinner: big carbs, early not late. 1,100
- Bed: dates + hot drink. 200

**Appetite test.** If food sounds bad at 8,360 ft, go liquid at Trail
Camp. Drink mixes and soup instead of a solid dinner.

Portal Store: if halal meat isn't confirmed, get pancakes, grilled
cheese, fries.

## sep 15, the carry

6 mi, 3,700 ft gain, full pack. Burns 4,000, target 3,400 in.

- 90 min out: 2 oat packets, protein scoop, honey, coffee. 600
- Moving: 50-70 g carbs/hr, 500-700 ml/hr. 900
- Arrival, within 30 min: ramen + protein scoop. 450
- Afternoon: tortilla + nut butter, dates, hot drink. Keep grazing. 500
- Dinner 17:00: veg entrée + olive oil. Early and bland. 950

Hip belt carries the weight, not the shoulders. Use eating stops as
scap resets.

Before bed: summit food into one jacket pocket, treat tomorrow's water.

## sep 16, summit and out

- 02:00 oats or cream of wheat, honey, half scoop. 300. Low fat, low fiber
- 02:45 depart. Pocket: 4 gels, 2 bars, 1 salty thing, tabs
- 03:30 first feed. Then every 45 min, 25-30 g carbs
- 05:00 Trail Crest. Sit 5 min, eat 200 kcal, drink
- 07:30 summit. 150-200 kcal even if nauseous
- 08:00 descent. Keep 30 g/hr. Bonks happen going down
- 10:30 Trail Camp. 400-500 kcal + protein, then break camp
- 11:15 pack out. 30 g/hr and 500 ml/hr the whole way
- 15:00 Portal, then real food in Lone Pine

## halal

- Freeze-dried: no certified meat versions. Use veg entrées, add a
  protein scoop or fish pouch.
- Jerky: skip it. Halal ships slow. Cover protein with cheese, nuts, fish.
- Chews and gummies: check for gelatin. Pectin chews and gels are fine.
- Cocoa: the marshmallow kind has gelatin. Buy plain.

## skip

- Untested food
- Alcohol from the 12th
- High fat, high fiber, spicy from the 15th on
- Big protein loads at dinner above 10,000 ft
- The 02:00 breakfast. You won't be hungry. Eat it anyway.

---

AMS, acetazolamide, headache or nausea that doesn't clear: doctor
questions, not fueling questions. Persistent symptoms mean descend.
Worth asking about now that this is one night at 12,000 ft, not two.
