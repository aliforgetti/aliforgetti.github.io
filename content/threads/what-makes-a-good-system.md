How do you define and create a good system?

<!-- came up 2026-08-03, while rebuilding this vault -->

## a first answer

Maybe a good system is one you actually use, and has as little friction
as possible.

One you can leave and then come back to and not feel like you have to
start all over again.

Not complex but pretty simple — and elegant at the same time.

Easy to set up in multiple mediums.
